Plan a room change with your own measurements and prices. This planner totals the items you add, applies optional tax and contingency percentages, and prepares a print-friendly estimate. It does **not** supply market prices or contractor quotes: enter the prices you have researched, and treat the result as a planning estimate.

Use it for storage systems, furniture, paint, organizers, lighting, or a small room refresh. Start with the items you know; add or remove lines as your plan changes.

**How the total is calculated:** each line is quantity × unit price. Tax is applied to the items subtotal, and contingency is then applied to the subtotal plus tax. Choosing “Other” for currency shows plain numbers with “(your currency)” and no symbol.
